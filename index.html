Appointment Process

How Your Appointment Reaches the Hospital

Every booking follows a transparent four-step workflow from patient request to hospital confirmation.

1

Sign In & Select Dept

Log in via Google or Email/Password, choose your specialty, doctor, and preferred date/slot.

2

Routed to Hospital Desk

Your request is stored in Supabase and routed to the selected department's hospital scheduling queue.

3

Slot Confirmation

Hospital staff verifies doctor availability and updates your status from Pending to Confirmed.

4

Visit & Consultation

Arrive 15 minutes early with your Booking ID for smooth OPD check-in and doctor consultation.
