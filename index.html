Appointment Process

How Your Appointment Reaches the Doctor

Every booking follows a transparent four-step workflow from patient request to doctor confirmation.

1

Sign In & Select Dept

Log in via Google or Email/Password, choose your specialty, doctor, and preferred date/slot.

2

Sent to Doctor Queue

Your request is saved in Supabase and sent directly to the doctor's appointment dashboard.

3

Doctor Confirmation

The doctor reviews your request and updates your status from Pending to Confirmed.

4

Visit & Consultation

Arrive 15 minutes early with your Booking ID for smooth OPD check-in and consultation.
